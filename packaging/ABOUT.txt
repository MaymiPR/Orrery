Orrery Suite
============

Orrery Suite is an open-source project (AGPL-3.0): https://github.com/MaymiPR/Orrery

It is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Cradle.
Product names belong to their owners and only describe what each plug-in is modelled on. None of their code, artwork,
preset files, fonts or other files is included: each plug-in was built from measurements of how the original sounds
and behaves, and its editor is drawn by Orrery Suite. Wraith's reverb plays room and plate responses measured from the
original's output.
