# Classification

The classification sheet of Wraith: one row per stage the reference's own power buttons isolate, plus the parameter
smoothing, the plate and the factory presets. Every number is that of [MEASUREMENTS.md](../MEASUREMENTS.md) or
[NULLTESTS.md](../NULLTESTS.md), and each cell names the section it comes from. The suite predates the structure
probe's result file, so no `structprobe.json` is published: the structure facts are those of the structure probe
table of MEASUREMENTS.md 1. Residuals are read on the scale of NULLTESTS.md: a null below -60 dB, close from -40 to
-60 dB, a model from -20 to -40 dB, not a null above -20 dB.

NULLTESTS.md also holds a track null (A mastered track: a full mix through the chain, a stress case for a vocal
chain; 15 settings, 4 a null, 0 close, 3 a model, 8 not a null) and a stress grid (Stress tests: 260 cases, median
-19.3 dB, worst -5.1 dB; 53 a null, 5 close, 69 a model, 133 not a null). The grid runs every stage alone, pairs,
presets, levels, random settings and automation at 48 kHz, block 512, pairs and levels at 44.1 and 96 kHz, and
levels and automation at blocks of 64 and 2048. Each row cites its own group. Two groups belong to no single row:
pairs (20 cases, median -18.3 dB, worst -11.2 dB) and random (48 cases, median -13.0 dB, worst -5.1 dB).
