# Classification

The classification sheet of Positron: one row per stage of the reference's chain, with its class, the measurement
that decided the class, the method, the target and the round budget. The first four rows are the isolation dicts of
the target file; the last four are stages without a dict of their own. Every number is that of
[MEASUREMENTS.md](../MEASUREMENTS.md) or [NULLTESTS.md](../NULLTESTS.md), and each cell names the section it comes from.
The words for a residual are those of NULLTESTS.md: a null below -60 dB, close from -40 to -60 dB, a model from -20
to -40 dB, not a null above -20 dB.
