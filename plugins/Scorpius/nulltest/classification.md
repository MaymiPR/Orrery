# Classification

The classification sheet of Scorpius: one row per stage that the reference's own power buttons isolate (`MODULES`,
`PARALLELS`, `ONLY` and `NONE` in `sc_ref.py`), and one row each for the expander, the rate converters, the smoothing
and the bypass. Every number is that of [MEASUREMENTS.md](../MEASUREMENTS.md) or [NULLTESTS.md](../NULLTESTS.md), named
by section in its cell; `results.json` is the published file `measurements/Scorpius/nulltest/results.json`. A figure
marked "(typed, no published file)" is one NULLTESTS.md gives from an earlier build: the row "low, tones and chords"
is the only one left. "Stress grid" names the section "Stress tests" of NULLTESTS.md (`null_stress.py`, 334 cases) and
"track null" its section "A mastered track" (`null_track.py`). No `structprobe.json` verdict
exists for any row (not recorded: the suite predates the tool); the Deciding measurement column names the measurement
that decides the class. The Target column takes its gates from the thresholds of the null-test checklist, and the
Round budget column the rule of the stage class. Status reads the residual on the scale of NULLTESTS.md: below -60 dB
a null, -40 to -60 dB close, -20 to -40 dB a model, above -20 dB not a null.
